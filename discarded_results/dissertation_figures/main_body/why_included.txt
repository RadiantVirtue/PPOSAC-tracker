MAIN BODY FIGURES — INCLUSION JUSTIFICATIONS
============================================

ppo_periodic_opposition_score.png
  RQ1 headline figure. This is the central metric of the dissertation, directly
  measuring whether the PPO policy gradient distinguishes between successful and
  failed episodes. Its trajectory over 3M training steps is the primary empirical
  evidence for answering the first research question, showing how clearly the
  agent's learning signal separates the two outcome groups throughout training.

ppo_periodic_coherence_combined.png
  Supports interpretation of the opposition score. Decomposing the signal into
  within-group coherence for success and failure separately allows the reader to
  assess whether opposition arises from genuine structural alignment within each
  group rather than random gradient cancellation, ruling out trivial explanations
  for high or low opposition values.

ppo_periodic_activation_cosine_distance.png
  The most reliable activation-space separation metric. Cosine distance is
  scale-invariant and does not inflate with the growth of activation magnitudes
  over training, making it directly comparable across training stages and
  potentially across algorithms. It provides honest evidence of representational
  separation between success and failure in the penultimate layer.

ppo_periodic_rsa_alignment.png
  RQ3 figure. Tracks how closely the agent's internal representations align with
  the ground-truth functional hierarchy of Crafter objects over training. This is
  the key evidence for whether learned representations acquire task-meaningful
  structure beyond what is needed for immediate action selection.

ppo_rdm_averaged_step0050000.png
ppo_rdm_averaged_step1550000.png
ppo_rdm_averaged_step3000320.png
  Visual evidence for the RSA alignment trend. Averaged RDMs at early, mid, and
  late training make the alignment score concrete and interpretable — the reader
  can see the representational geometry directly rather than inferring it from a
  single correlation value. Using averaged rather than per-seed heatmaps keeps
  the result clean and reduces seed-specific noise.

ppo_periodic_threshold_bounds.png
  Validates the success/failure partitioning scheme. Showing that the percentile-
  based EPS threshold grows steadily and maintains a consistent gap between upper
  and lower bounds throughout training confirms that the partition remains
  meaningful and does not degenerate, which is a prerequisite for trusting all
  other gradient and activation metrics in this chapter.

ppo_seed1_return_vs_opposition_score.png
  Connects the abstract gradient metrics to observable learning progress. By
  overlaying smoothed episode return with opposition score on a dual axis, this
  figure allows the reader to judge whether gradient distinction tracks actual
  improvement in task performance, grounding the analysis in the agent's behaviour
  rather than keeping it purely in weight space. Seed 1 is used as the canonical
  example; seeds 2–5 are in the appendix for replication.

ppo_periodic_summary_dashboard.png
  Compact cross-metric overview. A 3×3 dashboard covering all nine key metrics
  lets the reader survey the full analysis in a single figure and see whether
  different metrics tell a consistent story. It can also substitute for several
  individual figures if space is constrained, with the individual figures retained
  for the appendix as higher-resolution references.
